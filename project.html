Student Complaint Portal

A web-based portal that allows students to submit complaints and report lost items. Administrators can manage and track the submitted information through the system.

About the Project

The Student Complaint Portal provides an easy way for students to submit their complaints and lost item reports online. It also helps administrators manage complaints and keep track of their status.

Key Features

  • Student Registration and Login
  • Submit Complaints
  • Report Lost Items
  • View Submitted Complaints
  • Admin Dashboard
  • Manage Complaint Status
